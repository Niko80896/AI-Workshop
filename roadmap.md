# Roadmap
## What this is
October is for baseball fans who want to see an MLB prediction model's calls on playoff games before they are played. Signed-in visitors see each game's predicted winner and win percent, predicted first-five-innings (F5) leader, and projected total runs, and can like or dislike each prediction.
## What Done means
A stranger opens the live site, signs up with an email and password, and logs in. They see every game in the predictions table, soonest first, with its date, away team, home team, predicted winner and win percent, predicted F5 winner, and projected total runs. They can like or dislike each prediction, see the like and dislike counts on every game, and find their own reaction still highlighted when they come back. The site only reads and displays predictions; the model runs elsewhere and its output is loaded into the database by hand. Nothing beyond that is needed to call the project done.
